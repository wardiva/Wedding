# Barat redesign: research notes

Captured 11 Oct 2026 at 393×852 (touch). Screenshots are in `research/`.

## Sites reviewed

- **Enviory "Cinema"** (Indonesian Muslim platform; `01`, `02`): large serif names over a thin date rule. Akad Nikah and Resepsi each get a card with labelled Date / Time / Location rows.
- **Enviory "Foil Press"** (`03`–`05`): a full-bleed photo with blurred edges. Its timeline uses thin numerals on a hairline rail, and its dress code uses labelled swatches.
- **Enviory "Cartouche"** (`06`, `07`): script "The Wedding of" above an oval photo. Arch-topped venue card with an outlined Directions button; closest to our arch motif.
- **Invifest "Darshan"** (Indian; `08`, `09`): native-script headings, then English, then a framed date plate. The venue block has a solid gold "Open in Maps".
- **Invifest "Meenal"** (`10`): blessing line, italic invitation text, large names joined by a gold "&", parents in small italic.
- **Zola** (`11`): the premium templates are restrained: script plus spaced caps over photography.
- **The Knot "Black Tie"** (`12`): names on a solid dark band above the photo, so text never fights the image.
- **Joy demo** (`13`, `14`): generic. Each schedule item has Add to Calendar and Directions buttons.
- **Paperless Post**: only marketing pages are public, so no screenshots. Its cards use one dominant name line.

## Patterns to borrow

**(a) Hero video.** None of the legible examples put thin text on a busy mid-tone; they use a vignette or blur (Foil Press) or a solid band (Knot). Ours: a warm-dark bottom gradient (0 → ~80%), plus a backdrop blur masked to fade in from the bottom. Ivory script names with a soft shadow, and the date in spaced caps.

**(b) Invitation hierarchy.** Blessing, then italic lead-in, then event name, then names (largest), then date/time, on solid paper.

**(c) Venue.** One card: name, address, a filled primary button ("Open Location") and an outlined secondary ("Add to Calendar").

**(d) Schedule.** A vertical rail, time left and title right (Joy, Foil Press). Avoid Cinema's tilted tickets.

**(e) Dress code.** One clear word plus one guidance line; swatches optional.

**(f) Premium on mobile.** Whitespace. One accent colour. Hairline rules and small ornaments. Lining-serif numerals. Slow fade/rise reveals. One idea per screen.

## Avoid

- Text over an unscrimmed photo (`03` is borderline).
- Tilted cards.
- Sticky bottom navs and promo pills on a 393 px screen.
- More than three type styles per section.
- Saturated CTA colours (Joy coral, Knot pink).
- Long copy in script.
